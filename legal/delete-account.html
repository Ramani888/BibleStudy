Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Verdance (operated by Divyesh Ramani) lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly from within the app.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Verdance app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Prefer to request it by email?

If you cannot access the app, email us from your registered address at ddtechservices.work@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and we will process the deletion for you.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your personal data and all content you created, including: your name, email, and profile; your flashcard sets, cards, notes, and study plans; uploaded media; and your study progress and social data. This is completed within 30 days.

What may be retained

We do not keep your personal data after deletion, except where retention is required by law (for example, transaction or tax records held by our payment providers for the period required by applicable regulations). Such records are kept only as legally required and are not used for any other purpose.
