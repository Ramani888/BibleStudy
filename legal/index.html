Questions

Is Verdance free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you study, quiz, use every social feature, and ask AI questions with credits you earn from daily logins and streaks. Starter and Pro add monthly AI credits, media in chat, and more storage.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

Verdance is a study system, not a chat box. Flashcards, spaced-repetition review, quizzes, study plans, and group study — all tracked over time. The AI is one feature inside it, tuned for scripture with verse citations.

Can I study with my church or small group?

Yes. Create a group, share a study plan, and everyone follows the same steps while a group leaderboard keeps you encouraged. Social features are free on every plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are billed through the Apple App Store and Google Play, so you can cancel anytime right from your phone's settings — no hidden charges and no cancellation hoops.

Which devices does it support?

Verdance is coming soon to the Apple App Store and Google Play, for both iPhone and Android.
