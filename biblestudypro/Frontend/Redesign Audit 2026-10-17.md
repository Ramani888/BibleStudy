## Big picture
- **Structure is right.** Every screen sits in the right tab, tab-bar highlights match the real stacks, and nothing from
  one module has leaked into another. Home's shortcuts (Achievements, Leaderboard, Notes, Media…) exist in the real Home today.
- **Main problems are 3 kinds:**
  1. **Missing states** — error, loading, empty, out-of-credits, "someone else's set", confirmations. The canvas mostly shows the happy path.
  2. **Made-up data or promises** — numbers or features the backend doesn't have.
  3. **Hard-coded back buttons** — many go to one fixed screen, but the real app goes back to wherever you came from.
