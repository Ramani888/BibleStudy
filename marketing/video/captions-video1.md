## Where the clickable link goes (do this BEFORE posting)

A URL typed into a caption is **not clickable** on Reels, Shorts or TikTok. Put the tracked link where each
platform makes it tappable, then point the caption there.

| Platform | Clickable spot | Link to put there | Check in the app |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Profile **bio links** (Edit profile → Links → Add external link; up to 5) · **Story link sticker** | `https://getverdance.com/?c=ig-v1` | Your account shows "Links" under Edit profile; a Story with the link sticker the same day helps |
| **YouTube** | **Channel links** (YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info → Links; shown on the channel page/header). Links in Shorts descriptions and comments are not clickable. | `https://getverdance.com/?c=yt-v1` | Channel page shows the link under the channel name |
| **TikTok** | **Bio "Website"** field — only on a Business account, or once a personal account reaches the follower threshold | `https://getverdance.com/?c=tt-v1` | Only if you have legitimate access to post from your location; skip otherwise |

Note: one bio/channel link serves every video on that profile. When video 2 goes up, either keep the same link
(simple) or swap it to `…?c=ig-v2` the day video 2 posts (more precise per-video numbers).

The end card shows plain **getverdance.com** for people who type it — those visits show as untagged.
