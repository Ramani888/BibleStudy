## Where to read the results
- **App Store:** App Store Connect → Analytics → Acquisition → Campaigns (by code). Only counts once the
  provider token (`PT`) is set in the site and below. Apple hides very small numbers (under ~5) for privacy.
- **Google Play:** Play Console → Statistics / Store analysis → acquisition by UTM campaign.
- **In-app activity** (same for every campaign): PostHog `quiz_completed` (first practice), app opens
  (return visits), `referral_signup` (creator referral codes).
- Ask each creator to also share their in-app **referral code**. Their followers get bonus credits, and
  `referral_signup` tells you exactly who came from whom.
